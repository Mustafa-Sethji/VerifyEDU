## Why We Built VerifyEdu

As MCA students ourselves, we've sat through enough lectures to know the gap between "I attended the class" and "I actually understood it" is real — and invisible to teachers until an exam, when it's too late to do anything about it. VerifyEdu closes that gap: teachers upload their lecture material, and within seconds get a quiz — grounded strictly in that content — that measures genuine understanding, not just recall.
